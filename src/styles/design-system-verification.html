Anti-Slop Checklist

  • ✓ Tajweed colors are functional, not decorative
  • ✓ Arabic text uses line-height 2.0
  • ✓ Progress indicators use green, not generic blue
  • ✓ No fake metrics or placeholder stats
  • ✓ Empty states have actionable suggestions
