Bayan Logo Concepts

Three directions — geometric mark, integrated wordmark, and minimal typographic.

Option A — Geometric mark + wordmark

A standalone 8-point star circle mark paired with the wordmark. The mark can be used as a favicon/app icon. Green accent ties to the brand.

Option B — Integrated mark (star inside wordmark)

The "B" is constructed as a geometric form with star circles nested inside its counters. More distinctive, but harder to read at small sizes.

Option C — Pure wordmark with star accent

Cleanest option. The "dot" of the B is replaced with a green star circle. A thin accent line grounds the wordmark. Works at any size. Most versatile for favicon/app icon use.
